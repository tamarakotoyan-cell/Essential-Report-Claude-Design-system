Cards

Square corners, flat fills, no shadows. The header bar carries the label; the badge overlaps its right edge. Gold marks the public phase, slate and navy mark stakeholder phases.

.es-card--headed — the methodology phase card

Phase 1

Public

What?

Three-day online overtime discussion board with n=38 participants.

Why?

Exploratory qualitative research to understand existing attitudes and test where support for more funding sits.

Who?

50/50 split male and female. Mix of ages, states, incomes and household types.

Phase 2

Stakeholders

What?

Nine in-depth interviews, 45 to 60 minutes each.

Why?

To get feedback on the narrative frames before they were taken back to the public.

Who?

A mix of executive, political advisory and media advisory roles.

Phase 3

Refinement

What?

One-day online board with nine stakeholders reviewing the refined messages.

Why?

To understand how to optimise each message for government and for the public.

Who?

Communications and leadership roles across the eight commissions.

.es-panel — the message-testing verdict panel, with the pill straddling its top edge

Positive
  • Stakeholders believe this approach will gain traction with the public because fairness is a core part of the Australian identity.
  • The idea that access to justice is currently a privilege of the rich feels credible and concerning.
  • The phrase “justice delayed is justice denied” is powerful and memorable.
Improve
  • Some say to consider flipping the eight per cent statistic to put the emphasis on the limitation.
  • Some mention avoiding the implication that lawyers are motivated by money, as this risks undermining the message about fairness and service.

.es-flow — a logic sequence. Steps the audience can make read navy; steps they cannot read grey.

.es-stages — the four-stage method strip, ramping light to dark

Stage 1
Initial snapshot

Quant snapshot: a short survey run on the Essential poll.

Sample: n=1,041, nationally representative of the target population.

Stage 2
Exploratory qualitative

Six 90-minute live online focus groups with priority cohorts.

Sample: n=37 participants, recruited on their existing position.

Stage 3
Iterative testing

Two 90-minute live online focus groups testing the narratives.

Sample: n=11 participants drawn from the earlier cohorts.

Stage 4
Quantitative validation

Standalone poll (n=2,000+) to test the narratives at scale, measure resonance and size each segment.

The dot field

One colour at five opacity steps on a square grid. Density and opacity ramp together, so scattered points resolve into a solid block — evidence accumulating into a finding. The dense end goes at the outer edge, never under the copy.
